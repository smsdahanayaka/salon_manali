# Site Structure & Sitemap

**Confirmed (Step 1 of the site planning discussion, 2026-08-17): this is a
multi-page site.** Chosen specifically because separate pages let each one
independently target "Devinuwara, Matara" in its own title/H1 — directly
helping the naming-disambiguation problem the pitch is built around (see
[[competitor-research]] §3). Built as a speculative pitch demo — see
[[pitch-strategy]] and [[salon-manali]] memory §0.

Last updated: 2026-08-17

---

## Final page list (5 pages)

Deliberately kept to five — matches the "flat menu wins" pattern from
[[trends-and-inspiration]] §B.1, and avoids fragmenting a small single-branch
site into pages with too little real content each.

### 1. Home
Purpose: prove in one screen that this is a real, credible, local bridal
salon, and route everyone toward booking.
- Hero: one clear claim ("Bridal Dressing & Beauty — Devinuwara, Matara") +
  primary "Book Now" CTA above the fold, no scrolling required to find it
- Featured bridal work (photo strip/grid) — "show the work immediately"
- Short services overview (cards linking to Services)
- Trust signals: testimonials (see note below), any credibility marker
- Location/contact strip in the footer area: address, phone, hours, map —
  present on every page, not just Home

### 2. Services
Purpose: make the offering and (unusually for this market) the price range
clear enough that a visitor doesn't have to message just to get a ballpark.
- Bridal dressing & makeup — confirmed flagship, leads the page
- Hair & beauty services — marked tentative/placeholder until confirmed
  this branch offers them beyond bridal
- Pricing shown as illustrative "starting from" bands (placeholder, clearly
  marked) rather than "contact for pricing" — this alone would put the demo
  ahead of every Southern Province competitor researched

### 3. Gallery
Purpose: the single most important trust-building asset for a bridal
salon. For the demo: tasteful placeholder imagery only, never presented as
this salon's real work (per [[pitch-strategy]] §3).
- Grouped by style if/when volume allows (traditional / modern / mixed
  ceremony looks) — matches the segmentation pattern seen at ALEE, Salon
  Nayanart, and Eueni

### 4. About
Purpose: credibility + the disambiguation moment.
- The salon's story, who runs it, what makes this branch distinct
- Explicitly and consistently anchors "Devinuwara" — the natural place to
  make clear this is a specific, distinct salon, not one of the other
  same-named branches elsewhere in Sri Lanka

### 5. Book Now
Purpose: one destination that handles both quick contact *and* a proper
bridal inquiry — merged into one page rather than split, since splitting
"Contact" from "Bridal Inquiry" would leave two thin pages on a small site.
- WhatsApp click-to-chat + phone as the fast path (matches confirmed
  regional booking norms — [[industry-findings]] §5)
- A structured bridal inquiry form below that: wedding date, ceremony
  time, headcount, on-location vs. in-salon — mirrors the
  Trixies/Salon 253 Bride pattern from [[trends-and-inspiration]] §B.4
- Address, embedded map, hours (placeholder, marked TBC)

## Navigation (mobile-first)

- Sticky header: logo/name + hamburger menu (Home / Services / Gallery /
  About) + a **persistent, visually distinct "Book Now" button** that's
  never inside the hamburger — always one tap away, matching the
  "book-within-seconds" pattern from [[trends-and-inspiration]] §B.2
- Footer on every page repeats address/phone/hours/map, so contact info
  never needs more than zero extra clicks to find

## Where testimonials live

Homepage, not a separate page — per [[trends-and-inspiration]] §B.6
("testimonials on the homepage, not buried on a separate page"). With no
real testimonials yet, this section stays honestly empty/"coming soon" in
the demo rather than inventing quotes (see [[pitch-strategy]] §3).

## Deliberately excluded for now

- **Manali Academy / training courses** — no evidence this branch offers
  it; excluded pending confirmation once we reach the owner (memory item 4)
- **Multi-branch / location finder** — out of scope; single-location site
- **A separate "Contact" page** — merged into Book Now (see above) to
  avoid two thin, overlapping pages on a small site

## Open items this still depends on

- Real bridal photo volume/quality, once available — may eventually
  justify splitting Gallery by style into sub-sections
- Whether non-bridal services are offered — demo leads bridal-only,
  Services page structured to expand easily later
- Owner's actual preferred booking channel, once we can ask — WhatsApp is
  the strong default for now
