## 1. National "bar-setters" (Colombo/Kandy — set the industry standard)

Not direct competitors (too far from Devinuwara to compete for the same
brides), but useful as a benchmark for what a serious bridal salon web
presence looks like in this market.

| Salon | Notable | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **ALEE Bridal** (Kandy, Colombo, Negombo) | Full real site: Home/About/Services/Portfolio/Testimonials/Blog/Booking/Contact + persistent "Book Now." Fixed LKR prices for small beauty treatments, but bridal packages are "Request Quote" only. Multi-channel booking (form, phone, email, Instagram DM). Portfolio filtered by style (Kandyan/Western). Also runs a "mobile bridal salon" (artist travels to client) as a differentiator. | [aleebridals.com](https://www.aleebridals.com/) |
| **Salon Nayanart & Academy** (Kadawatha/Makola) | Dedicated AWARDS nav item ("Best Bridal Dresser 2022," "Best Bridal Makeup 2021" — World Beauty Awards USA), founder is a celebrity actress. Portfolio segmented by cultural bridal style (Kandyan/Indian/Hindu/Western). Clever pricing middle-ground: a **self-select budget-band quote form** (e.g. under Rs.100k up to over Rs.300k) instead of fixed prices. | [salonnayanart.com](https://www.salonnayanart.com/) |
| **Diva and Debonair** | Dedicated AWARD & RECOGNITION section + a **Destination Wedding** package page (travel+accommodation-inclusive). No pricing shown; leans on "easy on the pocket" language + promos. 4 testimonials with photos and professional titles (actress, model). | [divandebonair.com](https://www.divandebonair.com/) |
| **Salon Dhammi** (Maharagama) | Has a dedicated `/services-pricing.html` page — one of the only sites attempting a real public price list. Owner positioned as a bridal-dressing pioneer, "enhance don't change" philosophy. Site was intermittently unreachable during research — worth a manual re-check. | salondhammi.com |
| **Roots Salon & Academy** (Bambalapitiya, Colombo) | Salon + academy combo including a "Diploma in Bridal Dressing" — a common dual revenue model (services + training) at the established end of the market. | [rootssalonandacademy.com](https://rootssalonandacademy.com/) |
| **Neeliya Mendis Salons** | Best example of **mixed pricing disclosure**: fixed prices for small services (threading Rs.500, manicure Rs.2,500), "starting from" for mid-tier (hair colour Rs.5,000+), inquire-only for bridal. This three-tier model is probably the most replicable pattern for a small site that wants to look transparent without pricing itself into a corner on bridal work. 25+ years' experience cited as credibility. | neeliyamendissalons.com |
| **Ramani Fernando Salons** | The largest chain (15+ locations, franchises in Bangladesh/India). Nav includes Academy, Fashion Week, Careers, Collection, Salon Finder — the "big brand" architecture; aspirational reference only, not a realistic template for a single branch. | ramanifernando.com |

⚠️ "Champa Privé" could not be conclusively verified as a real business name —
closest match found is **Champi Siriwardana Salons** (Rajagiriya/Colombo,
[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/ChampiSiriwardena/), [SalonReserve](https://salonreserve.com/listing/champi_siriwardana_salons_pvt_ltd)).
Flag as possibly mis-transcribed if it comes up again — confirm the exact
name before citing it as a benchmark.

## 2. Southern Province — direct competitive set

These are the salons Devinuwara brides realistically compare against.

| Salon | Location | Notable | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eueni Hair & Beauty Salon** | Matara, Galle, Tangalle (3 branches) | **The only Southern Province salon with a true multi-page real website** — own branch pages ([Matara](https://euenisalons.com/branch-matara.php), [Galle](https://euenisalons.com/branch-galle.php), [Tangalle](https://euenisalons.com/branch-tangalle.php)), style-segmented galleries ([Kandyan Brides](https://euenisalons.com/kandyan-brides.php), [Modern Kandyan Brides](https://euenisalons.com/modern-kandyan-brides.php)), plus bridal dress-making, saree jackets, going-away frocks, accessory rental — broader "one-stop bridal" scope than most. Direct site fetch blocked (403); its [SalonReserve listing](https://salonreserve.com/listing/eueni-hair-&-beauty-salon) confirms: no pricing shown, "Send Message" contact form only, 6-photo gallery, zero reviews on that platform, open 7 days 8:30am–6pm. **This is the salon to study most closely** as the direct multi-branch regional leader. |
| **Salon Roo Bridal** | Matara/Urubokka | No independent website — only [SalonReserve](https://salonreserve.com/listing/roo_bridal) + Facebook. Full standard menu (bridal, makeup, hair, brows, mani/pedi, facials). Zero reviews on SalonReserve. |
| **Salon Sanjaya** | Matara | Same pattern — [SalonReserve-only](https://salonreserve.com/listing/salon_sanjaya_matara), phone/message-form booking, zero reviews, no pricing. |
| **Salon Bonitha** | Galle (+ Kottawa/Colombo) | Facebook-only but strong social proof: **96% recommend, 816 reviews on Facebook**. Runs a bridal-specific sub-brand, **"Bella Bridal, powered by Salon Bonitha"** — a brand-architecture idea worth borrowing in a lighter form (e.g. a "Bridal by Salon Manali" section/landing page). | [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/SalonBonitha/) |
| **Salon Mouni** | Devinuwara | Same town — direct hyperlocal rival. Facebook-only, ~1,215 likes, tagline "Feel Our Natural Beauty." |
| **Salon Shiny** | Matara | Facebook + [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/salonshiny_hair/) only, no website. **22 years of operating history**, consistently praised for wedding dressing/makeup — leans on longevity as an informal trust lever since it has no site to state it on. |

**Pattern:** none of the Matara-district salons (Roo Bridal, Sanjaya, Mouni,
Shiny, Bonitha) has an owned website. **Eueni is the only Southern Province
salon with a real multi-page site**, and even it hides all bridal pricing.
This is the core opportunity — see [[industry-findings]] §4 for the full
differentiation analysis.

## 3. The "Salon Manali" name-confusion problem

This is bigger than initially scoped — at least **9-10 unrelated businesses**
nationally use the name "Salon Manali," several with far larger followings
than our client:

| Branch | Scale | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Kurunegala/Katunayake** ("Salon Manali Bridal Pvt Ltd") | **98,657 Facebook likes** — the biggest and most search-dominant of the group | [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/salonmanalikurunegala/) |
| **Nittambuwa** ("Salon Manali (Pvt) Ltd") | **71,619 Facebook likes** | [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/SalonManaliPvtLtdNittambuwa/) |
| **Galle** | ~10,754 likes, phone 0778809606 | [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/salonmanaligalle/) |
| **Kadawatha** | Appears to be the registered "Salon Manali (Pvt) Ltd" HQ, 4.5/5 (31 reviews) | [pro.makeup](https://pro.makeup/salon/sri-lanka/kadawatha/salon-manali) |
| **Middeniya** | Same general area (Hambantota/Matara border) as our client | [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/Salon-Manali-100057176241060/) |
| Gelioya, Ampara/Uhana, Gonawala | Smaller, found via directory search | Facebook |
| **Our client — Devinuwara/Matara** | The site this project is for | facebook.com/p/Salon-manali-100063475393190 |

🚩 **Critical finding:** even Facebook's own location tagging for our
client's page indexes it as **"Salon manali \| Matara"** — generic to the
whole district, not the specific town — even though the salon's own post
content explicitly says "Devinuwara." **The client's own current online
presence is already contributing to the disambiguation problem it needs the
new site to solve.**

**Implication for the build:** page titles, H1s, meta descriptions, and
schema.org LocalBusiness markup need to aggressively and consistently anchor
"Devinuwara," "Tanagalle Road," and "Matara District / Southern Province" —
not just "Matara" or "Salon Manali" alone — plus a full postal address and
embedded map, specifically because the Kurunegala (98.6K) and Nittambuwa
(71.6K) namesakes will otherwise dominate any bare "Salon Manali" search
nationally. Recommend a consistent tagline pattern everywhere (site, Google
Business Profile, social bios): **"Salon Manali — Devinuwara's Bridal &
Beauty Salon."**

## 4. Vendor directories & booking platforms — viability as listing channels

| Platform | What it is | Viability |
|---|---|---|
| **[SalonReserve](https://salonreserve.com/)** | SL-wide salon booking/directory SaaS, hundreds of listings, gives each salon a subdomain + booking flow + salon-facing CRM tools | Worth a free listing for SEO/backlink value, but regional listings (Roo Bridal, Sanjaya, Eueni) all show **zero reviews** — supplementary channel, not a real booking engine. |
| **Viwaha.com** ("Sri Lanka's No.1 Online Wedding Magazine") vs. **Wiwaha.com** | Two distinct, confusingly similar-named SL wedding-vendor directories — an ironic direct parallel to our own naming-confusion problem | List on viwaha.com (more consistently cited as the leading marketplace); confirm which domain is currently more active before investing in both. |
| **iWedding.lk** | Bridal-dressing-and-beauty category directory | Repeatedly cited alongside Viwaha as a standard bridal-vendor directory — reasonable to list. |
| **Fresha** | International booking platform, real SL presence, strongest actual instant-booking UX found | Skews toward Colombo — check Matara-district penetration before investing setup time. |
| Salon.lk, SalonApp.lk, SaloBook | Smaller local booking-platform entrants | Thin/JS-heavy, likely newer/smaller players — low priority, monitor only. |

**Bottom line:** none of these platforms substitute for an owned website +
a strong, correctly-configured Google Business Profile — see
[[industry-findings]] for why Google/Maps reviews look like the real
battleground, not these directories.
